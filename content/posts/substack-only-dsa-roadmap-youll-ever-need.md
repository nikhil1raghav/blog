## Why does this exist?

Life has too many good things. DSA prep is not one of them.

But you need to clear DSA interviews to get a tech job.
Most of the roadmaps out there are link spams with bazillion boring problems.

DSA is not going anywhere for 3-4 years at least. As it is the easiest way to scale the interview pipeline.

In other words it is the easiest way to filter candidates out on raw problem solving skills.

Hi I’m Nikhil, currently work as an SDE2 at Uber, I think I’m decent at DSA, have got offers from Google, Uber, Amazon, Dream11 and Flipkart. I have taken a lot of DSA rounds as interviewer too.

I just like to post good no fluff tech content. Not going to sell you a course.

Over time I’ve realised your capability to get a tech job boils down to two things

1.  Getting an interview

2.  Clearing an interview

DSA is 90% of the interview for a new grad and this post is about how to prepare for that.

## Three skills that matter

So learning DSA and getting dangerously good at clearing interviews has three parts

1.  Fundamental knowledge

2.  Pattern recognition (what every influencer keeps harping on when they shove their 15 pattern list down your throat)

3.  Speed (you’ve to solve at least two problems in an hour for most interviews)

# Roadmap begins

### Fundamentals: Read the CP handbook

Pickup competitive programmer handbook.

<https://cses.fi/book/book.pdf>

Yes competitive programmer “handbook”, no youtube videos.

Don’t come at me that you don’t like reading books, you have to do a lot of reading as a software engineer. Make reading dry stuff a habit.

This is the cleanest no fluff high quality DSA primer out there.

With very good code examples.

Don’t be scared of the name “competitive programming”

Author of this book also created this gold problemset

called CSES problemset [CSES - CSES Problem Set - Tasks](https://cses.fi/problemset/)

Read the book and solve problems from this problemset.

Google or chatgpt any problem you don’t understand.

I hate how easy it is to prepare these days.

I had to stare at random japanese blog like this just to understand a solution.

You’re still not very ready for facing the harsh world of Big tech interviews.

## Pattern recognition and speed

Now comes pattern matching and speed.

> There is only one way to get good at pattern matching and solve problems fast and that is not “solving sheets with the title of the pattern on top”

**when you know you’re going to solve a DP problem, you just look for a DP solution**.

You never get that feeling of despair where you don’t have an idea how do I attack this.

Therefore the best way is to do “virtual contests.”

> Virtual contests are replay of a contest just for you. It lets you take a past contest under the same strict time limits

Leetcode has a very good virtual contest support.

Spam a lot of virtual contest. Just open leetcode and start a virtual contest.

Doing 3 virtual contest in a week is good sweet spot. You’ll feel confident and will develop real “pattern matching” skill.

### Supplementing with sheets

Now, armed with all this knowledge, there is just one issue.

You didn’t solve binary tree reversal, linked list jumping problems.

Which is easy to fix by solving those problems from some sheet like [Striver’s](https://takeuforward.org/interviews/strivers-sde-sheet-top-coding-interview-problems/) or [algomasters](https://blog.algomaster.io/p/15-leetcode-patterns).

Honestly they’re not asked that much but prepare yourself.

#### Few days before the interview

Now just few days before interview go to this [repository](https://github.com/liquidslr/leetcode-company-wise-problems)

and solve medium or hard problems from the company that you’ve interview at. You’re done.

Think out loud during interview. The interviewer can’t read your mind. Silence looks like being stuck, but talking through a wrong idea shows signal and gives them a chance to nudge you back on track.

## Recap

Solve CSES problemset, read CP handbook.
Solve virtual contests for speed.
Company wise problems before the actual interview.
